Gilded — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Gilded ("the app") is a memory card-matching game made by Naj Studio. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no in-app purchases. It does not request internet access or any device permissions.

Data stored on your device

Your stars, scores, streaks and settings are saved only on your device so you can continue playing. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. We never receive it.

Daily challenge

The daily table is generated on your device from today's date. Nothing is uploaded and there is no online leaderboard.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You can delete all game data at any time with Settings › Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same address and in the app, with a new effective date.
